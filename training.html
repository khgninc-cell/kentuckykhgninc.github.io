⚠️ NOTICED DISCLOSURE (Arms-Length Operating Rule):
Kentucky History and Genealogy Network Inc. (KHGN Inc.) operates strictly as an independent, sovereign educational organization. KHGN Inc. maintains no formal joint ventures, legal partnerships, or institutional working relationships with local county historical societies, local registries, or external lineage clubs. Advanced archival training workshops, digitization lectures, and technical presentations are made available to local historical societies entirely on an elective, autonomous request-and-choice basis.

📋 Mobile Presentation & Deployment Mission

Our core operational focus is centered straight on independent, on-site historical data presentations and mobile preservation methodology workshops across the Commonwealth of Kentucky. We deploy directly into regional workshop facilities to provide advanced, un-cloggable technical tools for genealogical record organization.
