🚗 Kentucky History and Genealogy Network Inc.

Welcome to Our Digital Network Center

Kentucky History and Genealogy Network Inc. (KHGN Inc.) is a legally registered non-profit organization dedicated to preserving, indexing, and validating regional historical data, land boundaries, and ancestral lineages across the Commonwealth. We are actively structuring our lightweight, high-performance serverless cloud infrastructure to better serve our researchers, volunteers, and the public interest.
